# Plan evidence-led upskilling

Use gaps to improve both capability and credible evidence. A keyword appearing
once is not automatically a market need, and completing a course is not the
same as professional mastery.

## Workflow

1. Define the comparison set: one archived posting or a dated group of target
   roles. Preserve source and retrieval dates.
2. Compare requirements with verified, adjacent, conflicted, and missing
   evidence. Do not treat omitted CV content as a confirmed skill gap.
3. Group equivalent terms and quantify recurrence without mixing frequency,
   importance, and current proficiency.
4. Prioritise by target relevance, gap size, learning cost, and the strength of
   evidence the work can produce.
5. Research current primary learning sources, prerequisites, time estimates,
   and realistic proof projects. Label estimates and recommendations as such.
6. Propose a small sequenced plan, then let the user choose before scheduling or
   changing their profile.
7. After completion, record the actual artifact or assessment as evidence; do
   not claim proficiency beyond what it demonstrates.

Write dated private reports below `evidence/upskilling/`. If the user explicitly
connects another typed workspace, use its evidence and target operations
instead of creating a second source of truth. Do not buy a course, enrol,
schedule time, or publish a credential without an explicit instruction for that
external action.

Use `../../../templates/upskilling.md` as the output contract. Every priority
must retain its sources, confidence, effort estimate, and intended proof.
